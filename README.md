# 🛡️ ScamShield

> **AI-powered digital payment scam detection and risk intelligence platform.**

### 💡 Why ScamShield?

Digital payments have made everyday transactions faster and easier, but they have also created new opportunities for **online scams, fraudulent transactions, and social-engineering attacks**.

ScamShield is being built to address this problem by developing an intelligent system that can **analyze suspicious payment activity, identify potential scam patterns, and provide risk insights before a transaction can cause significant loss.**

The idea is simple: **make digital payments safer by detecting suspicious behavior early.**
